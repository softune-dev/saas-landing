# Softune Social Posts — v2 (cutout/collage style, 1:1)

Approved formula: a real lifestyle photo as full-bleed background, with 3-4 flat cutout "sticker" elements floating on top — each with its own border and drop shadow so it reads as layered above the photo, not blended in. Logo sticker top-center, headline sticker (Bangla headline + English support line) in the open space above the subject's head, one small supporting sticker (product/stat/status card, English text only), CTA sticker bottom-center (Bangla). Same subject face-clearance rule and color palette on every one.

**Palette:** Brand Orange `#FF5733` · Ink Black `#0F0F0F` · Muted Grey `#6B7280` · Surface White `#FAF9F6`

**Shared rule on every prompt below:** the subject's face stays in the lower two-thirds of the frame with open space above for the headline sticker; all 3-4 stickers have their own crisp border and visible drop shadow, distinct from the photo; small supporting-card text is always English, never Bangla; headline is Bangla with a colored accent phrase, its English support line is smaller and in Muted Grey; CTA sticker text is Bangla.

---

## 1. Run Your Store From Your Phone

```
Create a 1080x1080px square social ad photo in a layered collage/composite
style — a real lifestyle photograph as the full-bleed background, with 3
separate flat cutout sticker elements floating on top of it, each with its
own crisp white or colored border and a visible drop shadow so it reads as
cut out and layered above the photo, not blended into it.

BACKGROUND PHOTO: a young Bangladeshi shop owner sits in a small home
workspace or shop counter, warm natural window light, looking down at
their phone with a relaxed confident smile, one hand near a stack of
folded clothing or small packaged products beside them. The subject's face
stays in the lower two-thirds of the frame, open space above their head
for the headline sticker.

STICKER 1 — LOGO, top-center, small, floating just above the photo's top
edge: a rounded white pill containing a rounded orange square icon next to
the wordmark "Softune" in Ink Black.

STICKER 2 — HEADLINE, in the open space below the logo, a rounded white
card with its own drop shadow. Bangla line, large bold Ink Black:
"আপনার দোকান চলবে আপনার ফোনেই" — the words "আপনার ফোনেই" colored Brand
Orange. English line beneath, smaller, Muted Grey: "Products, orders, and
your whole storefront."

STICKER 3 — PRODUCT CARD, small, lower-left corner, overlapping the
photo's edge: a rounded white card with a tiny product thumbnail, the
English words "Cotton Tee" in Ink Black, and a small Brand Orange badge
with the English word "Live" in white. All text on this card is English.

STICKER 4 — CTA, bottom-center, floating just below the photo's bottom
edge: a bold rounded Brand Orange pill with a drop shadow, containing the
Bangla text "৩ দিন ফ্রি ট্রায়াল" in white, with a small white
right-pointing arrow icon.

Colors: Brand Orange #FF5733, Ink Black #0F0F0F, Muted Grey #6B7280,
Surface White #FAF9F6. Mood: warm, authentic small-business energy,
realistic photography with natural light — the stickers are the only
flat/graphic elements.
```
